The 3 Insurance Billing Models (DBA, DBM, DBC)

In commercial insurance, premium cash can be billed and collected by three different parties: the **Retail Broker (DBA)**, the **MGA (DBM)**, or the **Carrier (DBC)**. Here is a clear, step-by-step breakdown of how each model works and its exact double-entry accounting hits.

Model 1 Agency Bill

Model 1: DBA (Direct Bill to Agency / Agency Bill)

Run in Simulator

Flow Sequence: Insured → Broker ($10k) → MGA ($9k) → Carrier ($8.5k)

In DBA, the Retail Producer (Agency) issues the invoice directly to the customer. When the customer pays $10,000 cash, the Broker deducts their 10% commission ($1,000) and remits $9,000 net to the MGA. The MGA takes a 5% override ($500) and remits $8,500 net-net to the Carrier.

Model 2 Direct to MGA

Model 2: DBM (Direct Bill to MGA)

Run in Simulator

Flow Sequence: Insured → MGA ($10k) → Carrier ($8.5k) & Broker Comm ($1k)

In DBM, the Managing General Agent invoices the customer directly and receives the full $10,000 payment. The MGA then acts as the central settlement hub: wiring $1,000 to the Broker for retail commission and $8,500 to the Carrier for net underwriting settlement.

Step-by-Step Journal Entries (DBM):

2200 Premium Payable — Carrier $8,500.00
2150 Commission Payable — Broker $1,000.00
6100 Commission Revenue — MGA Override $500.00
2. Insured Pays MGA Directly MGA Ledger 1001 Cash / Bank (MGA Trust) $10,000.00
1100 Premium Receivable — Insured $10,000.00
3. MGA Pays Broker Commission MGA Ledger 2150 Commission Payable — Broker $1,000.00
1001 Cash / Bank (MGA Trust) $1,000.00
4. MGA Remits Carrier Settlement MGA Ledger 2200 Premium Payable — Carrier $8,500.00
1001 Cash / Bank (MGA Trust) $8,500.00
Model 3 Direct to Carrier

Model 3: DBC (Direct Bill to Carrier)

Run in Simulator

Flow Sequence: Insured → Carrier ($10k) → MGA ($1.5k Comm) → Broker ($1k Comm)

In DBC, the Carrier invoices the customer directly and collects the full $10,000 payment into its operating account. The Carrier then disburses a $1,500 combined commission remittance to the MGA. The MGA retains $500 for its override and forwards $1,000 to the Retail Broker.

Step-by-Step Journal Entries (DBC):

1100 Premium Receivable — Insured $10,000.00
3. Carrier Disburses Commissions Carrier Ledger 6100 Commission Expense — Broker $1,000.00
6101 Commission Expense — MGA Override $500.00
1001 Cash / Bank (Carrier Operating) $1,500.00
4. MGA Receives & Passes Broker Comm MGA Ledger 1001 Cash / Bank (MGA Operating) $1,500.00
6100 Commission Revenue — MGA Override $500.00
1001 Cash / Bank (Disbursed to Broker) $1,000.00
